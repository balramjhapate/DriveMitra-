# 01_Business_Research

# Purpose

This document defines the business foundation for the entire platform.
Every design, advertisement, landing page, and SEO decision must align
with this document.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Positioning

The platform is a lead-generation and booking platform that connects
customers with verified rental partners.

Current Phase: - Rental Partner Network

Future Phases: - Own Fleet - Multi-city Expansion - Marketplace

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Services

## Core Services

-   Self Drive Cars
-   Car Rental With Driver
-   Airport Pickup
-   Airport Drop
-   Outstation Taxi
-   Local Taxi
-   One Way Taxi
-   Round Trip

## Future Services

-   Monthly Rentals
-   Luxury Cars
-   Tempo Traveller
-   Bike Rental

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Customer Problems

-   Doesn't know which rental company to trust.
-   Wants transparent pricing.
-   Wants instant booking.
-   Wants WhatsApp support.
-   Wants clean, insured vehicles.
-   Wants quick confirmation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Value Proposition

-   Verified rental partners
-   Multiple vehicle options
-   Fast booking
-   Transparent communication
-   Responsive support
-   Mobile-first experience

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Customer Journey

1.  Search on Google
2.  Click Google Ad
3.  Reach landing page
4.  See trust signals
5.  Fill lead form or call
6.  Booking confirmation
7.  Vehicle allocation
8.  Customer support

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Trust Elements Required

-   Google Rating
-   Customer Reviews
-   WhatsApp Support
-   Phone Support
-   Clean Vehicle Images
-   Booking Confirmation
-   Transparent Policies

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Model

Primary: - Commission per booking

Secondary: - Lead generation partnerships

Future: - Own fleet income - Subscription packages - Corporate contracts

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Public Market Findings

Based on research and uploaded material:

Demand categories: - Self Drive Cars - Car Rental - Taxi Service -
Airport Taxi - Outstation Taxi

Frequently observed vehicle demand: - Ertiga - Swift - Baleno - Brezza -
Innova - Scorpio

Customer intent is highest for: - Immediate booking - Same-day
availability - Transparent pricing - WhatsApp contact

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Competitor Strengths

-   Strong Google Maps presence
-   Phone-first booking
-   WhatsApp support
-   Local trust

# Competitor Weaknesses

-   Outdated websites
-   Weak branding
-   Limited animations
-   Poor UI
-   Weak conversion flow
-   Generic copywriting

Opportunity: Build a premium modern experience focused on trust and
conversion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Rules

-   No fake discounts
-   No misleading pricing
-   Mobile-first
-   Fast loading
-   Clear CTAs
-   Real imagery
-   Honest policies

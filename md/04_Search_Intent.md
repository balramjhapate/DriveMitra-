# 04_Search_Intent.md

# Purpose

This document defines the exact search intent strategy that will drive:

-   Landing Page Architecture
-   Google Ads Campaigns
-   Meta Ads
-   SEO Content
-   CTA Placement
-   Conversion Optimization

This project follows **Intent First, Design Second**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Search Intent Hierarchy

## Level 1 --- Transactional (Highest Priority)

These users are ready to book.

Examples:

-   self drive car bhopal
-   self drive car rental bhopal
-   car rental bhopal
-   rent car bhopal
-   taxi service bhopal
-   airport taxi bhopal
-   outstation taxi bhopal
-   ertiga on rent bhopal
-   innova on rent bhopal

Landing page goal:

-   Immediate trust
-   Instant lead form
-   Phone call
-   WhatsApp click

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Level 2 --- Commercial Investigation

Users are comparing providers.

Examples

-   best self drive car bhopal
-   cheapest car rental bhopal
-   self drive vs taxi
-   car rental reviews

Required sections

-   Why Choose Us
-   Customer Reviews
-   FAQ
-   Trust Badges
-   Fleet

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Level 3 --- Informational

Examples

-   how self drive rental works
-   documents required
-   self drive rules

These are future SEO blog opportunities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Hero Requirements

Above the fold must answer:

-   Do you provide self drive?
-   Do you provide taxi?
-   Available today?
-   WhatsApp available?
-   Call available?
-   Which city?
-   Is booking quick?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CTA Strategy

Primary CTA

-   Book Now

Secondary

-   WhatsApp

Third

-   Call Now

Sticky CTA on mobile.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Trust Signals

Must appear before user scrolls.

-   Google Rating
-   Verified Partner
-   Instant Booking
-   Transparent Pricing
-   Multiple Vehicle Options
-   Support

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Content Rules

-   No keyword stuffing.
-   Natural language.
-   Location relevance.
-   Semantic keyword coverage.
-   Human-first copy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Conversion Goals

Primary: - Lead Form Submission

Tertiary: - Phone Call

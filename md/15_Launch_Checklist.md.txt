This document is the final production checklist before launching the website and running Google Ads.

The objective is simple:

**Launch once. Launch correctly.**

Every item below should be verified before spending money on advertisements.

# Project Completion

When all documents (00–15) are implemented:

- The website should be production-ready.
- The codebase should be AI-friendly and developer-friendly.
- The landing pages should support Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, and future expansion.
- New city pages and service pages should be easy to create using the same architecture.

# 12_Meta_Ads_Blueprint.md

# Purpose

This document defines the complete Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) strategy for the Car Rental platform.

The objective is NOT to generate likes or followers.

The objective is

- High Quality Leads
- WhatsApp Conversations
- Instant Bookings
- Low Cost Per Lead
- Brand Awareness
- Retargeting
- Repeat Customers

This blueprint is designed for

- Facebook
- Instagram
- Messenger
- WhatsApp
- Meta Advantage+
